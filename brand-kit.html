01 · Logo

The mark & wordmark

Our mark is the tree of life, roots and branches, growth and grounding. It carries the brand on its own and locks up beside the wordmark. Give it room: keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the height of one root system.

05 · Voice

Like a thoughtful editor

Knowing but not academic. Warm without being twee. Confident, direct, grounded in real specifics. Sentence case everywhere, never Title Case, never ALL CAPS in running text.

We sound like

  • "Eight to fourteen days inside one emerging market, building alongside the people already solving real problems."
  • "Curiosity and commitment matter most. If you'd rather build than observe, you'll fit right in."

We never sound like

  • "🚀 Disrupt your career and join the next-gen founder movement!"
  • "Unlock your potential and crush it with our exclusive global accelerator."
We say

founder · fellow · cohort · host · partner · fieldwork · immersion · emerging market · ecosystem · practice · place

We don't say

disrupt · hustle · grindset · killer · crushing it · rockstar · gamechanger · synergy · unlock · "future of , "
