What is Founders Without Borders?

Our programs place you inside an emerging entrepreneurial ecosystem and connect you with the people who understand it from the inside.

You'll meet founders, investors, operators, mentors and potential partners, while experiencing the culture, markets and businesses shaping the region.

Learn from founders

Workshops and conversations with the founders and local leaders building solutions in their communities.

Go into the field

Site visits to social enterprises, innovation labs, and impact hubs across the city.

Build a social venture

Work in small teams to identify a real problem, test ideas, and develop a venture from the ground up.

Intern for credit

Continue with a 4–6 week, credit-bearing internship at a startup or impact organization in Cape Town.
