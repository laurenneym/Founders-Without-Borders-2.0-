Why this program

A community-first model for changemakers

Cape Town is one of Africa's most dynamic entrepreneurial ecosystems, and this is your way inside it. You'll work alongside South African impact businesses and social enterprises, learn from the founders already solving real problems, and get hands-on experience building a social venture from the ground up. Walk away with the kind of perspective no classroom can offer.

It's a peer-to-peer immersion designed to develop the next generation of impact entrepreneurs, rooted in the communities and stories that actually shape South Africa.

Who it's for

Purpose-driven U.S. college students and recent grads passionate about real-world impact, and eager to develop solutions through community-first innovation. No prior experience required. Curiosity and commitment matter most.

What's included
  • Accommodation in well-located Cape Town neighborhoods
  • All meals and ground transportation throughout
  • Curated site visits with impact businesses and social enterprises
  • Daily workshops and mentorship sessions
  • Evening networking events and cultural experiences
  • Comprehensive travel support and local guidance

Worry-free booking

Reserve for just $100. Interest-free monthly payment plans and flexible cancellation on the balance up to 30 days before departure.

Why go

Cape Town brings a lot to the table.

One of Africa's most dynamic entrepreneurial ecosystems, set against Table Mountain, two oceans, and a national park within reach. You'll spend your days alongside the founders and local leaders building real solutions, and your evenings inside the culture that shapes them.

What you'll get
  • 8–14 nights in handpicked accommodations
  • 8 breakfasts, 2 lunches & 3 dinners
  • All in-country ground transportation
  • Curated visits with impact businesses & social enterprises
  • Daily workshops & mentorship
  • 24/7 in-country support

The experience

Three ways you'll go deep

Real impact work

Collaborate, don't observe

Work with South African impact businesses and social enterprises leading initiatives in startups, VCs, sustainable fashion, community development, and food security.

Cultural immersion

Beyond tourism

Step into the communities, traditions, and stories that shape South Africa's entrepreneurial spirit, and learn how place and culture drive innovation.

Innovation & entrepreneurship

Build your network

Meet and learn from local social entrepreneurs and business leaders building real ventures, and find your place in a community that keeps building together.

Day by day

Your journey

Day1

Arrival & orientation

Welcome to Cape Town. Arrive, settle into your accommodation, and gather for an orientation dinner with the cohort and local guides, the start of the network you'll build throughout the trip.

Days2–3

Urban innovation

Step inside the city's startup ecosystem. Visit hubs and accelerators, meet founders and investors, and understand the local context through guided tours and hands-on workshops.

  • Highlights
  • Startup-hub and accelerator visits
  • Founder & VC conversations
  • Sustainable-fashion and impact-business tours
Days4–5
Immersion

Community immersion

Engage directly with community leaders and active development projects. Participate in meaningful work alongside social enterprises in food security, community development, and education.

  • Highlights
  • Social-enterprise site visits
  • Food-security & school-feeding partners
  • Collaborative, hands-on project work
Days6–7

Deep dives & reflection

Intensive workshops on impact measurement, scaling social ventures, and systemic change. Reflect on what you've learned with your cohort and mentors before you carry it home.

  • Highlights
  • Innovation-lab visit
  • Impact, ESG & scaling workshop
  • Guided cohort reflection
Days8–13
Extended track · optional

Go further

Advanced mentorship, networking opportunities with impact leaders across different industries, and personalized deep-dive projects with partner organizations, plus more time in the field.

  • Highlights
  • Advanced mentorship
  • Networking with impact leaders across industries
  • Partner deep-dive projects
FinalDay

Closing & next steps

Celebrate with your cohort, present your key learnings, and step into an alumni network and ongoing engagement that keeps building long after you fly home.

  • Highlights
  • Capstone share-out
  • Certificates & recognition
  • Farewell dinner
